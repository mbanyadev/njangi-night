# Njangi Night 🪙

**Save together. Bid smart. Sew your future.**
A bright, short (10-minute) financial-literacy game about the **njangi**, the rotating savings circle used by millions across Africa and the world (tontine, susu, jam'iya, chama…).

▶ **Play:** https://mbanyadev.github.io/njangi-night/

You're a young tailor in Bamenda. Each month you contribute to the pot, survive life's surprises, and decide whether to **bid** for the pot early (borrowing, with a price) or **wait** (earning a share of everyone else's bids). Buy the sewing machine, build your Trouble Jar, avoid Quick Cash, and keep your neighbours' trust.

- Game design doc: [docs/GDD.md](docs/GDD.md)
- Budget: [docs/BUDGET.md](docs/BUDGET.md)

Built for the Zoud GameLab game jam by **Mbanya Dev**. Vanilla HTML/CSS/JS, no build step. Open `index.html` or serve the folder.
